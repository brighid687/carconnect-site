Trade-in quote

A fair price for your current car.

Our quoting system values your car against live listings of the same model and registration year in Singapore's used-car market. No guesswork, no lowball opening offer.

  • Live market dataPriced against what comparable cars are listed for today, not a rate card from last quarter.
  • Your remaining COE and rebates countedThe paper value left in your car is built into the quote.
  • Checked by a personEvery quote is reviewed by our team before it's final, and confirmed when we see the car.
