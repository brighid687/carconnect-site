Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how CarConnect SG Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202318036M), the sales and marketing arm behind Dongfeng Experience, collects and uses your personal data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What we collect

  • When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or at the Experience Centre: your name, contact number, your messages, and what you tell us about the car you're looking for, such as the model, when you plan to buy, whether you have a trade-in and your preferred language.
  • When you ask for a trade-in quote: your name, mobile number, car plate number, the make, model and registration year of your car, and the model you're interested in.
  • When you test drive or buy a car: the details and documents the purchase needs, such as your NRIC, driving licence and contact details, and, if you apply for financing, the documents the finance company asks for.
  • When you apply for a job: your CV and the details you send us.

This website

  • No cookies or analytics of our own. We don't track your visit.
  • The trade-in form doesn't store anything on this website. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and you choose whether to send them to us.
  • Instagram posts on this page are shown using Instagram's own embed. When they load, Instagram (Meta) may collect information such as your IP address and set cookies, under Meta's privacy policy. You can block third-party cookies in your browser settings.
  • Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps take you to those services, which handle your data under their own terms.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange an Experience Centre visit or test drive.
  • To prepare your trade-in quote.
  • To prepare, process and complete your purchase, registration, insurance and financing, if you go ahead.
  • To consider your job application.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Who we share it with

We share your personal data only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Meta (WhatsApp), which carries the messages you send us on WhatsApp under its own terms.
  • When you buy or trade in: finance companies, insurers, the Land Transport Authority and the other parties needed to complete the sale or the transfer of your car.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry and trade-in details while your enquiry is open, and review them when it closes. We keep purchase records for as long as the law and our contracts require. We keep job applications only for as long as we need them to consider your application, unless you agree to us keeping them for future roles. We dispose of personal data once we no longer need it.

Offers and updates

We may sometimes send you news and offers about our cars by WhatsApp, SMS or phone. We do this only with your consent, and we check the Do Not Call Registry where the law requires it. You can ask us to stop at any time, and we will.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been used or disclosed in the past year, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to our using it. Withdrawing consent may mean we can't continue to help with your enquiry or purchase.

We'll update this notice before we change how we handle your data. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
